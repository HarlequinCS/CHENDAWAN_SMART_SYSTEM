The Company and the Contractor are hereinafter collectively referred to as the “Parties” and individually as a “Party”.

1. Engagement and Scope of Work

1.1 The Company hereby engages the Contractor, and the Contractor accepts such engagement, to perform the services and deliver the tasks set out in Appendix A (Scope of Work) for the project known as [Insert Project Name] (“Project”).

1.2 The Contractor shall perform the services in a professional, diligent, and workmanlike manner and in accordance with any timelines, specifications, and instructions agreed in writing by the Company.

1.3 Any work outside Appendix A shall require the Company’s prior written approval and may be subject to additional fees.

2. Independent Contractor Status

2.1 The Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor and not as an employee, worker, agent, partner, or joint venturer of the Company.

2.2 Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating a contract of service, employment relationship, or entitlement to employee benefits.

2.3 The Contractor shall be solely responsible for all personal income tax obligations and any statutory obligations applicable to the Contractor’s own business or status. The Contractor shall not be entitled to paid leave, medical benefits, bonuses, EPF contributions, SOCSO, EIS, or any other employee benefits unless required by law or expressly agreed in writing.

3. Term

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until completion of the Project or earlier termination in accordance with this Agreement.

3.2 The Parties may extend or modify the Project term by written agreement.

4. Fees and Payment

4.1 In consideration of the services, the Company shall pay the Contractor a total fee of RM [Total Amount], subject to the successful completion of the milestones stated in Appendix A.

4.2 Unless otherwise stated in Appendix A, the Company shall pay approved invoices within 7 days of written approval of the relevant deliverables.

4.3 The Contractor shall submit invoices in the form reasonably required by the Company, together with supporting evidence of milestone completion where applicable.

4.4 The Company may withhold payment for any deliverable that is incomplete, non-conforming, defective, or not reasonably acceptable under Appendix A.

4.5 Unless expressly approved in writing, no expense incurred by the Contractor shall be reimbursable by the Company.

5. Deliverables and Acceptance

5.1 The Contractor shall deliver the work product in the format, quality, and timeline stated in Appendix A.

5.2 The Company shall have a reasonable period to review each deliverable and may reject any deliverable that does not materially comply with the agreed specifications.

5.3 If the Company identifies defects, omissions, or non-conformities, the Contractor shall remedy the same within a reasonable time at no additional charge, provided the issue arises from the Contractor’s failure to comply with the agreed scope.

6. Intellectual Property Rights

6.1 Upon full payment of all fees due under this Agreement, all work product specifically created by the Contractor for the Project, including source code, documentation, reports, designs, and other deliverables, shall become the exclusive property of the Company, to the extent permitted by law.

6.2 The Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all rights, title, and interest in and to such work product, including copyright and other intellectual property rights arising from the Project.

6.3 Notwithstanding Clause 6.1, the Contractor retains ownership of pre-existing materials, reusable tools, templates, libraries, methods, know-how, and background intellectual property developed independently of the Project.

6.4 To the extent any Contractor background materials are incorporated into the deliverables, the Contractor grants the Company a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use such materials for the Company’s business purposes in connection with the Project.

6.5 The Contractor shall not use the Company’s name, logo, or deliverables for marketing, portfolio, or promotional purposes without the Company’s prior written consent.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Contractor shall keep strictly confidential all confidential information, source code, vulnerabilities, client data, business operations, security findings, technical information, and any other non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of the Company.

7.2 The Contractor shall use such confidential information solely for the purpose of performing the services under this Agreement and shall not disclose it to any third party except with the Company’s prior written consent or as required by law.

7.3 This confidentiality obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement.

8. Data Security

8.1 The Contractor shall take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of any Company or client data accessed during the Project.

8.2 The Contractor shall promptly notify the Company of any actual or suspected unauthorised access, disclosure, loss, or compromise of data or systems.

9. Return of Property

Upon completion or termination of this Agreement, the Contractor shall promptly return or securely destroy all Company property, documents, records, credentials, data, and materials in the Contractor’s possession or control, except as otherwise required by law.

10. Termination

10.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving 7 days’ written notice to the other Party.

10.2 The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the Contractor:

  • materially breaches this Agreement;
  • fails to deliver the agreed work;
  • commits misconduct, fraud, negligence, or wilful default; or
  • breaches confidentiality or data security obligations.

10.3 If the Contractor terminates before completion, or if the Company terminates due to the Contractor’s failure to deliver or breach, the Company may withhold pending payments and/or pay only for usable work satisfactorily completed up to the termination date.

11. Warranties

The Contractor represents and warrants that:

  • the Contractor has the skill and authority to perform the services;
  • the deliverables will not knowingly infringe third-party rights;
  • the Contractor will perform the services in compliance with applicable laws; and
  • the Contractor will not introduce malicious code, backdoors, or unauthorised access mechanisms into any deliverables.

12. Limitation of Authority

The Contractor shall have no authority to bind the Company, incur liabilities on behalf of the Company, or make representations on behalf of the Company unless expressly authorised in writing.

13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Malaysia. The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Malaysia.

14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with Appendix A, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the Project and supersedes all prior discussions, understandings, or arrangements relating to the same subject matter.

15. Amendment

No amendment or variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

16. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

17. Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one instrument.
