TEAM CHENDAWAN VENTURES (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia (“PDPA”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal data when you engage our services, visit our website, contact us, or otherwise interact with us.

1. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect personal data including, without limitation:

  • your name;
  • email address;
  • telephone number;
  • company name and job title;
  • billing, invoicing, and payment details;
  • correspondence and communication records;
  • technical data such as IP address, device information, access logs, and system logs; and
  • any other information you voluntarily provide to us in connection with our services.

Where applicable, we may also collect information relating to your use of our websites, web portals, software solutions, or cybersecurity and IT services.

2. Purpose of Collection and Processing

We collect and process personal data for the following purposes:

  • to provide, manage, and deliver our IT consultancy, software development, web development, cybersecurity, and related technical services;
  • to communicate with you regarding projects, support requests, quotations, proposals, and service updates;
  • to issue invoices, receipts, quotations, and other administrative or financial documents;
  • to conduct security audits, monitoring, troubleshooting, maintenance, and system support where permitted by agreement;
  • to comply with legal, regulatory, contractual, and internal policy requirements; and
  • to improve our services, processes, security controls, and client experience.

3. Disclosure of Personal Data

We do not sell or trade personal data. We may disclose personal data only where necessary for the purposes above, including to:

  • employees, contractors, and advisers who need the information to perform their duties;
  • hosting providers, cloud service providers, payment processors, and other technology vendors engaged by us;
  • auditors, accountants, insurers, legal advisers, and professional consultants;
  • regulatory authorities, law enforcement agencies, or other parties where required or permitted by law; and
  • other third parties with your consent or as otherwise permitted under applicable law.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures may include access controls, encryption, secure storage, and system monitoring.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention of Personal Data

We will retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, to comply with legal or regulatory requirements, or to protect our legitimate business interests where permitted by law. When personal data is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to destroy or permanently delete it in accordance with applicable requirements.

6. Access and Correction

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of your personal data held by us. If you believe any personal data we hold about you is inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated, you may contact us using the details below and we will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

7. Provision of Personal Data

Where personal data is required for us to provide services, process payments, issue documents, or comply with legal or contractual obligations, failure to provide such data may affect our ability to perform the requested services or enter into a contract with you.

8. Cookies and Technical Logs

Where applicable, our website or systems may use cookies, log files, analytics tools, and similar technologies to improve functionality, monitor performance, maintain security, and analyse usage trends. You may adjust your browser settings to limit cookies, but some features may not function properly as a result.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. The updated version will take effect on the date stated at the top of the policy when published or otherwise communicated to you.
